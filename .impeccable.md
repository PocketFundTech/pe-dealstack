## Design Context

### Users
Private equity and M&A deal teams: partners, principals and associates at lower mid-market funds and advisory shops. They open Avise at the start of the day and between calls to see where every live mandate stands, what's due, and what needs a decision. They read numbers for a living and have no patience for decoration that doesn't carry information.

### Brand Personality
Exacting, discreet, ledger-bound. The interface should feel like a well-set daily deal sheet from a private bank or a financial newspaper: calm, confident, precise. Emotional goal: *control*. The user should finish scanning the page knowing exactly what matters today.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Refined banker**, light theme (users work in lit offices during market hours). Banker Blue `#003366` stays the anchor hue. Neutrals are tinted toward it (OKLCH hue ~252).
- References: the FT and WSJ markets pages, deal tombstones, annual-report financial statements (hairline rules, double-rule totals, tabular figures).
- Anti-references: generic SaaS admin templates, rows of identical stat cards, colourful donut charts, dark mode, purple/teal "AI" gradients, big rounded icon tiles.
- Type: **Inter, product-wide**. This is a founder decision (2026-09-27): one font across the product. A Bodoni Moda + Schibsted Grotesk pairing was tried and rejected. Hierarchy comes from weight (600 for headings and figures), tight tracking, size contrast and tabular figures, not from a second typeface.

### Design Principles
1. **Numbers first.** Figures are the content. Set them large, tabular, right-aligned, and never decorate them.
2. **Rules, not boxes.** Separate with hairlines and whitespace before reaching for cards and shadows.
3. **One colour family.** Stage and category data use a Banker Blue ramp. Red means overdue or error only, and green means done or positive only.
4. **Say what's true.** Copy reports real state ("3 in diligence, 1 overdue"). No filler like "AI market analysis" when there is none.
5. **Quiet motion.** A single staggered reveal on load, plus bars that grow from zero. Everything respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
