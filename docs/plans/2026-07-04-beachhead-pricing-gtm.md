### Recommendation: independent sponsors + first-fund PE firms (US lower middle market)

Narrows the S42 target rather than replacing it. Mid-market firms are the destination ($30K ACVs) but not the beachhead: post Feb-2026 DealCloud+ToltIQ partnership they get bundled AI diligence, and displacing an incumbent with zero references and no SOC 2 is the chasm, not the beachhead.

- **Burning pain (5/5):** 2–4 person teams see 200–500 teasers/CIMs a year with no analysts. CIM triage, spreading, and IC memos are done by partners nights-and-weekends. Extraction + memo builder + firm research attack exactly this. Current stack: Gmail + Excel + Dropbox.
- **WTP (3/5):** No ops budget line, but deal math dominates — one closed deal = seven-figure promote. $200–500/mo firm-level is an easy yes. Expect $3–8K ACVs, not $30K. Beachhead's job is references and product truth, not revenue.
- **Winnable (5/5):** DealCloud starts ~$30–50K/yr; funded AI-DD startups (Meridian, DiligenceSquared, Keye) hunt upmarket. Nobody owns this segment. Dominant mindshare in 12 months is realistic.
- **Referral (5/5):** Sponsors co-invest, share deal flow, cluster in iGlobal + McGuireWoods events, Searchfunder, tight LinkedIn circles. And they *graduate* into committed funds — carrying the product upmarket.

**Revenue reality:** 25–40 firms × $3–8K ≈ $100–250K ARR in 12–18 months. The point is manufacturing the references, case studies, and security posture that make the $30K-ACV segment winnable.

**Expansion roadmap:** (1) beachhead 0–12mo → (2) sub-$500M committed funds 9–18mo (start SOC 2) → (3) S42 core target $300M–$1B at 18–30mo ($30K ACV, $1M+ ARR path) → optional self-serve ETA tier.

## Part 2 — Value-Based Pricing Strategy

### The value unit is the deal, not the seat

| Value driver | Customer math |
|---|---|
| CIM screening + spreading | 3–6 analyst-hrs/CIM @ ~$100/hr loaded; 300 CIMs/yr = $90–180K of triage labor |
| IC memo drafting | 20–40 hrs/memo; 40% reduction = thousands per deal |
| Headcount avoidance | Alternative is hiring an analyst: $150K+/yr deferred |
| Throughput → promote | One incremental deal = seven-figure promote; near price-insensitive at SaaS price points |

Alternatives priced: DealCloud ~$30–50K+/yr before ToltIQ; AI-DD startups reportedly $50K+/yr enterprise contracts (**unverified — confirm in discovery calls**).

Seat pricing (S42's $99–249/user/mo) fails here: beachhead has 2–4 people (caps ACV where value/person is highest), seats penalize adoption, and AI that replaces labor should price against output, not logins.

### Recommended model

**Firm-level tiers + deal-analysis allowance (hybrid flat + usage), free wedge. Value metric: deals analyzed/month. Seats unlimited within fair use** — a marketing weapon vs DealCloud ("priced per deal, not per seat").

| Tier | Price | Target | Included | Positioning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Free — CIM Analyzer** | $0 | Anyone with a CIM | 2 CIM analyses/mo, summary + red flags, watermarked export | Acquisition wedge (S42 rec) |
| **Sponsor** | $299/mo ($249 annual) | Independent sponsors, searchers, Fund I | 10 deal analyses/mo, memo builder, CRM + pipeline, 25GB VDR, ≤5 users | Beachhead tier → ~$3–3.6K ACV |
| **Fund** ⭐ | $999/mo ($833 annual) | Sub-$500M funds | 30 analyses/mo, firm research agent, email capture, SSO, audit logs | Anchor tier → ~$10–12K ACV |
| **Firm** | from $2,500/mo (~$30K/yr) | $300M–$1B AUM | Custom allowance, SOC 2 report, custom templates, API, dedicated support | Half of DealCloud+ToltIQ → the $1M ARR path |

- **Overage:** ~$35/additional deal analysis, soft-capped (warn, never hard-stop mid-deal).
- **Founding program:** first 20 firms, 40% off locked 24 months, in exchange for logo + quote + monthly feedback call. Replaces the old "$99 founding" (which anchors permanently low).
- **Sanity check vs S42 seat pricing:** 3-person sponsor @ $99/seat = $297/mo ≈ Sponsor; 15-person firm @ $149/seat = $2,235/mo ≈ Firm. Roughly revenue-neutral at endpoints, but removes seat friction and adds a usage expansion lever. Gross margin 83–88% (S42) absorbs LLM COGS; re-verify with real usage.

### Sensitivity hypotheses (test in 10 discovery interviews)
- Sponsor: too cheap <$100/mo (toy signal), good value $250–500, resistance >$750.
- Firm: $20–50K/yr reads normal vs DealCloud; <$10K/yr may hurt credibility.

### Experiments / assumptions / risks
- **Experiments:** $299 vs $499 Sponsor quotes across founder-led calls; pricing-shown vs talk-to-us for Firm; track free→paid + deals-analyzed distribution to tune allowances.
- **Assumptions → tests:** deal volume 10–30/mo per firm → measure in free tier; competitor prices → ask prospects their quotes; unlimited seats safe → fair-use clause + monitor.
- **Risks → mitigations:** usage anxiety → generous allowances + soft caps; free cannibalization → 2/mo cap, gate memo builder + VDR; procurement demands seat pricing → offer as alternative quote at equal-or-higher total, never lead with it.

## Part 3 — GTM Plan: First 10 Paying Customers

### Positioning & messaging

**Core message:** *"The AI analyst for deal teams that don't have analysts. Screen every CIM, draft IC memos in hours, and run your pipeline in one place — priced per deal, not per seat."*

| Against | Message |
|---|---|
| DealCloud + ToltIQ | "Enterprise price, enterprise implementation. PE OS is live the same day, at a tenth of the cost." |
| Meridian / DiligenceSquared / Keye | "They sell AI diligence to firms that already have platforms and analysts. PE OS is the whole stack for firms that have neither." |
| Status quo (Excel + Dropbox + Gmail) | "You didn't raise a fund to spend Saturdays spreading financials." |

**Proof assets (all in flight):** Luktara interactive demo deal, security whitepaper + trust page (`/security`), no-training-on-your-data pledge, founding-customer case studies.

### Channel selection (few channels, done excellently)

| Channel | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Free CIM Analyzer (product-led wedge)** | Primary | Zero-trust-required first touch; the CIM is the pain; converts strangers into activated leads |
| **LinkedIn founder-led content + targeted DMs** | Primary | The entire segment lives there; India→US friction is zero; 3 posts/wk (build-in-public + CIM teardowns + deal-math content) |
| **Searchfunder + sponsor communities** | Primary | High-trust watering hole for exactly this buyer; near-zero cost |
| **PE industry advisor (advisor equity)** | Force multiplier | S42 action item; solves the trust + network gap for an unknown solo founder; recruit by month 2 |
| iGlobal / McGuireWoods events | Later (month 4+) | Expensive travel from India; meanwhile mine attendee/speaker lists for outbound |
| Paid ads | No | Tiny audience, brutal CPCs, no repeatable funnel yet |

**Timezone note:** US East 9–11am = 6:30–8:30pm IST — a clean daily founder-sales window; protect it.

### Funnel math to 10 customers

Outbound: 300 precisely-targeted sponsors (Searchfunder + LinkedIn Sales Nav + event attendee lists) → ~10% to call (30 calls) → ~50% to trial (15) → ~2/3 close at founding rate = **10 paying**. Inbound from the CIM Analyzer (target 150 signups in 90 days) runs in parallel and should contribute 2–4 of the 10.

### 90-day roadmap

- **Weeks 1–2 (readiness):** finish security P0 branch, publish whitepaper + trust page, ship Free CIM Analyzer, finish Luktara demo deal. *Nothing else ships until these do.*
- **Weeks 3–4 (validation):** 10 discovery interviews (double as first sales calls; test $299 vs $499); start LinkedIn cadence; build the 300-target list.
- **Weeks 5–8 (sales):** outbound waves of 25/wk; demo → founding offer; target first 5 paying. Begin advisor search.
- **Weeks 9–12 (momentum):** 2–3 case studies into Searchfunder/LinkedIn; referral ask baked into onboarding ("who else should have this?"); customers 6–10; advisor signed.

### KPIs & go/no-go

| Metric | 90-day target |
|---|---|
| CIM Analyzer signups | 150 |
| Discovery/sales calls | 30+ |
| Paying firms | 10 (≈$40K ARR at founding rates) |
| Signup→call | ≥10% |
| Call→paid | ≥30% |
| Cash CAC | ~$0 (founder time only) |

**Day-60 checkpoint:** <3 paying firms → stop and diagnose (message? segment? trust? product?) before spending weeks 9–12; re-run discovery rather than pushing harder on a broken funnel.

### Risks

- **Trust gap** (unknown vendor + confidential CIMs) — the #1 risk. Mitigate: security page, DPA template, no-training pledge, SOC 2 roadmap published, advisor's name.
- **Solo-founder bandwidth** — sales eats eng. Mitigate: timebox selling to the IST-evening window; freeze non-GTM feature work for 90 days.
- **Free-tier abuse / COGS** — 2/mo cap, watermarks, monitor token cost per analysis.
- **Competitor bundling accelerates** — speed is the defense; the beachhead segment is beneath their radar for now.
